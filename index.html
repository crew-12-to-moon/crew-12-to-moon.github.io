Now

I'm a founding engineer at Arctus Aerospace, where I work on avionics and system architecture for unmanned aircraft. That means designing PCBs and writing the low-level software that holds the system together: scheduling, memory management, communication between boards and subsystems, failsafe logic and telemetry.

Our flight control is built on an open-source autopilot. My part is extending it for what we need, like redundancy and support for new subsystems, rather than reinventing it.

Some days that's code, some days it's a soldering iron, and once it was three nights with a logic analyzer and an engine that didn't want to talk.

Alongside Arctus, I'm presenting my swarm research at AIAA SciTech 2027.
